About

Engineering with data at the core

I'm Srinaath, an Artificial Intelligence and Data Science undergraduate at Dr. N.G.P. Institute of Technology, Coimbatore. Most of my work sits where product engineering meets intelligence: I design the interface, write the API, model the data, and then make the system learn something useful from it.

On the full-stack side I build with React, FastAPI, Spring Boot and PostgreSQL — shipping role-based auth, RESTful services, real-time inventory flows and dashboards that stay fast under real data. On the machine learning side I've built hybrid LSTM + XGBoost forecasting pipelines, NLP summarisation systems and automated profiling for messy datasets.

Data analytics is the thread through all of it: cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis and visual storytelling in Power BI, Pandas, Plotly and Matplotlib. What I enjoy most is problem solving — reducing a vague business requirement to a well-shaped model, schema and screen that a real person can use without a manual.
